FIRST USER/TASK TEST - PROPOSED, NOT CONDUCTED

Hypothesis: a deliberate gaze/dwell selection may help a frequent hands-busy task
where an existing phone/mouse/keyboard/voice method is awkward. The first user,
buyer and task are not yet validated. The synthetic message flow is a convenient
software experiment, not proof that messaging is the right market.

BEFORE SHOWING A SOLUTION
Ask the person to describe the last real occurrence, frequency, current workaround,
what errors cost, who chooses/pays for tools, and why existing options fall short.
Record concrete examples and refusals as carefully as positive comments.

FORMATIVE COMPARISON
1. Choose one harmless repeatable task with synthetic data, a credible baseline
   and success/error definitions agreed before looking at the result.
2. Begin with desktop mouse/keyboard and the declared synthetic-replay mode only
   as software checks. A real gaze comparison needs physical camera calibration
   and independent targets first. Do not treat replay as participant eye data.
3. Counterbalance task/condition order where practical. Keep data and task equal.
4. Capture completion, elapsed task time, unintended highlights/selections,
   cancellations, retries, calibration time, perceived effort and desire to return.
5. Test loss/reconnect, look-away and deliberate confirmation in a harmless context.
6. Use coded participant IDs and appropriate permission. Avoid employer/customer
   messages, identifiers or patient records. No recruitment is authorized here.

INTERPRETATION
An exploratory small sample helps identify failure modes; it does not establish
population efficacy, market size or clinical benefit. A cofounder's enthusiasm
also does not establish customer demand. Decide continue/narrow/pause based on
observed advantage, acceptable errors and willingness to repeat the task.
